# Customer_behavior_analysis
Data analytics project showcasing customer behavior analysis using SQL, Python and Power BI

# 🛒 End-to-End E-Commerce Sales & Customer Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a complete end-to-end data analytics workflow to extract, clean, analyze, and visualize customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, category trends, and regional profitability. The pipeline leverages **SQL** for business querying, **Python (Jupyter Notebook)** for exploratory data analysis (EDA) and data preprocessing, and **Power BI** for interactive visualization.

---

## 🛠️ End-to-End Tech Stack & Workflow
* **SQL:** Extracted transactional insights by answering 10 complex business and customer behavior questions[cite: 3].
* **Python (Jupyter Notebook):** Data cleaning, missing value treatment, feature engineering, and EDA using pandas, numpy, and visualization libraries.
* **Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX measures creation, and building an interactive analytical dashboard.

---

## 🐍 1. Python (Data Preprocessing & EDA)
* **Tasks Performed:** Loaded raw datasets, handled missing values, cleaned column formats, and performed exploratory data analysis (EDA) to understand distributions before querying and visualization.

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions Addressed (SQL Analysis)
To uncover customer behavior patterns, the following queries were executed in SQL
1. What is the total revenue generated by Male vs. Female customers?
2. Which customers used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount?
3. Which are the top 5 products with the highest average review rating?
4. Compare the average Purchase Amounts between Standard and Express Shipping.
5. Do subscribed customers spend more? Compare average spend and total revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers.
6. Which 5 products have the highest percentage of purchases with discounts applied?
7. Segment customers into New, Returning, and Loyal based on their total number of previous purchases, and show the count of each segment.
8. What are the top 3 most purchased products within each category?
9. Are customers who are repeat buyers (more than 5 previous purchases) also likely to subscribe?
10. What is the revenue contribution of each age group?

# 🛒 Customer Behavior Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Power BI Dashboard** designed to analyze customer purchasing behavior, category-wise sales performance, subscription adoption rates, and demographic trends. The objective is to extract actionable insights to optimize marketing campaigns, improve customer engagement, and boost revenue.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary & Key Metrics (KPIs)
* **Total Customers:** 3.9K
* **Average Purchase Amount:** $59.76
* **Average Review Rating:** 3.75 / 5

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Business Recommendations

### 1. Category Performance
* **Insight:** **Clothing** is the primary revenue generator ($104K revenue, 3,403K sales) followed by **Accessories** ($74K revenue, 2,412K sales). Footwear and Outerwear contribute lower sales.
* **Action:** Maintain robust inventory for Clothing and Accessories while deploying targeted discounts and bundling strategies for Footwear and Outerwear to lift sales

### 2. Subscription Status Analysis
* **Insight:** A significant majority—**73% of customers—do not have a subscription**, with only **27% subscribed**[cite: 6].
* **Action:** Launch targeted email campaigns, exclusive discounts, or free-shipping offers for non-subscribers to convert them into loyal subscribed members.

### 3. Demographic Insights (Age Groups)
* **Insight:** **Young Adults** are the top-performing demographic, contributing the highest sales (1.03K) and revenue ($62K), closely followed by Middle-Aged customers.
* **Action:** Tailor digital marketing and trending fashion promotions toward Young Adults via social channels to maximize conversion rates.
